About

Tools that stay on your device.

TextMatch Studio is a small collection of free browser tools for short-form video and social media creators. The idea is simple: the flashy edit should take seconds, not an afternoon in After Effects.

Our tools

Fast cuts between newspaper-style frames while one highlighted word stays fixed in the center.

Freeze Frame Video Maker

Freezes one or more moments and adds a record scratch, captions, zoom and a rewind.

Open tool →

Why it exists

Editors and creators usually build these effects by hand, which takes a long time for a short clip or a single thumbnail. These tools do the layout, timing and cutout for you, so you only supply the word, the photo or the clip.

How it is built

Everything runs in your browser. The match cut tool draws every frame on an HTML canvas and encodes the video on your device. The text behind image tool finds the subject of your photo with an AI model that runs locally. The freeze frame tool decodes your video, adds the effects and encodes the result on your device. There is no server-side processing, which is why your words, photos and videos stay on your device and why the tools are free to use.

Who makes it

It is built and maintained by an independent video creator. Feedback and feature ideas are welcome on the contact page.
