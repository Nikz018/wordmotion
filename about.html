About

TextMatch Studio is a small collection of free browser tools for short-form video and social media creators.

Our tools

Text Match Cut Maker makes text match cut videos: fast cuts between newspaper-style frames while one highlighted word stays fixed in the center. Text Behind Image places text behind the subject of a photo, so the subject appears in front of the lettering.

Why it exists

Editors and creators usually build these effects by hand, which takes a long time for a short clip or a single thumbnail. These tools do the layout, timing and cutout for you, so you only supply the word or the photo.

How it is built

Everything runs in your browser. The match cut tool draws every frame on an HTML canvas and encodes the video on your device. The text behind image tool finds the subject of your photo with an AI model that runs locally in your browser. There is no server-side processing, which is why your words, photos and videos stay on your device and why the tools are free to use.

Who makes it

It is built and maintained by an independent video creator. Feedback and feature ideas are welcome on the contact page.
