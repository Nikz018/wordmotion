Privacy · Last updated 11 October 2026

Your files never leave this tab.

Your words, photos and videos stay on your device. The word you type, the photo you upload and the video or image you generate are processed entirely in your browser. We do not upload, store or see them.

Text Behind Image tool

This tool finds the subject of your photo with an AI model that runs in your browser. The first time you use it, your browser downloads the model and its supporting files from public content delivery networks (jsDelivr and Google Cloud Storage), and the text fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web request, this lets those providers see your IP address and standard browser details. Your photo itself is never sent to them or to us.

Freeze Frame Video Maker

Your video is decoded, edited and encoded entirely in your browser. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. This tool loads its text fonts from Google Fonts, which, like any web request, lets Google see your IP address and standard browser details.

Advertising

This site shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can choose personalized or non-personalized ads in the banner, and manage your preferences at adssettings.google.com. Learn more in Google's advertising policy.

Local storage

We store your ad-consent choice, appearance preference, and optional recent match-cut words in your browser’s local storage. Nothing else is stored.
